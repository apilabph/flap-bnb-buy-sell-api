# Integration research ? October 13, 2026

Target users are bot and wallet developers who need a single inspected transaction across curve and graduation stages. Direct launchpad contracts and DEX routers are free alternatives. Our added value is canonical venue checks, an atomic fee, absolute net-output minimums, partial-fill refunds, simulation and an inspectable local-signing flow. These are implemented safeguards, not claims of universal uniqueness or verified subscriber demand.

The portfolio's volume study found sustained venue activity before selecting this project: Flap: $106.59m over seven days and $537.90m over thirty days. These are historical observations from the portfolio research, not API demand or earnings forecasts. Begin with a public beta and measure real integrations; hosting, RPC and support costs can exceed fees without adoption.

Reviewed primary sources: [version specification](https://docs.flap.sh/flap/developers/wallet-and-terminal-and-bot-developers/token-version-specification), [trade interface](https://docs.flap.sh/flap/developers/wallet-and-terminal-and-bot-developers/trade-tokens), [migration caveats](https://docs.flap.sh/flap/developers/wallet-and-terminal-and-bot-developers/token-migration), [Tax Token V3](https://docs.flap.sh/flap/developers/basic-and-mechanism/flap-tax-token/tax-token-v3). Asymmetric taxes must be read separately. The creation-time commission receiver does not grant our trading API another launcher?s tax revenue. Our settlement instead collects its own visible fee on native trade value.

Native BNB pairs only. Stablecoin/ERC20-quoted launches are not covered; observed recent activity includes these, so total venue volume overstates our initial addressable routes. Canonical V2/V3 routing and current protocol restrictions are validated during simulation. No broad support, cheapest-price or fastest-execution claim is made.
